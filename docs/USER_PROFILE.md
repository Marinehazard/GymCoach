## 4. Progression rules

- **Top set:** one triple at **RPE 8** (2 reps left). Increase only when it feels like RPE 7.
- **Back-off sets:** 3 sets, rep progression **5 → 6 → 7**, then +5 kg and back to 5s.
- **Accessories:** double progression — hit the top of the rep range on all sets, then go up.
- **Barbell lifts:** never above RPE 8. **No max singles** outside test weeks.
- **Failure:** allowed only on the last set of isolation / machine work.
- **Cycle:** 6 weeks, then a test week on the lift closest to target.
- **Plates:** home gym has no 1.25 kg plates → 5 kg jumps; use 1.25s when available.
- **Known habit to watch:** overshooting — maxing instead of working sets, adding reps beyond plan.

**RPE scale:** 10 = no rep left · 9 = 1 left · 8 = 2 left · 7 = 3 left.

## 9. Technique cues

**Squat**
- Bar over midfoot, high bar, elbows down, toes out 15–30°
- Setup under 10 s: unrack, two steps, settle, one breath, go
- "Spread the floor" — knees track over toes
- "Chest up, knees forward" through the middle third (fixes hips rising first)
- Film from the open side of the rack at hip height

**Bench**
- Blades pinned together and down, slight arch, feet planted under knees
- Forearms vertical at the bottom; bar to lower sternum, elbows 45–60°
- Press up and back toward the rack (J-curve), leg drive
- Safeties set 2–3 cm below the chest with arch

**Overhead press**
- Bar on collarbones, forearms vertical, glutes squeezed, ribs down
- Head back to let the bar pass, then push the head through; bar finishes over midfoot

**Deadlift**
- Moderate load on return; RPE 8 cap
- No heavy deadlifts within 4 days of a squat test

**Face pulls:** pulley at face height or above, pull horizontally, elbows high, pull the rope apart.
**Straight-arm pulldown:** face the stack, stand back so the cable angles, sweep to the thighs.

## 10. Known issues & fixes

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Left knee drifts in under heavy squats | Banded walks before the bar (+5 left), Bulgarian split squats (left first), abductor, hip thrust |
| Hips rise first out of the hole | Pause squats, hack squat (quad strength), "chest up, knees forward" |
| Long setup under the bar | <10 s routine |
| Overshooting (maxing, extra reps) | Top set is the set; singles only in test weeks |
| Tight grip / elbows after arm work | Forearm flexor + extensor stretches |

## 12. Recovery & nutrition

- Protein: **1.6–2 g per kg bodyweight daily**
- Sleep is the biggest recovery lever; long work days are the main threat
- Pre-workout: coffee 30–45 min before (2 cups max), carbs 60–90 min before (e.g. pancakes with jam/honey/banana, low fat); within 30 min only a banana or toast
- Post-workout: protein within 1–2 h
- Tired after a hard work day → rest, don't force it
